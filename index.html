About QuickKB Image Resizer

QuickKB Image Resizer is a free online tool designed to help users resize and compress images to a desired file size. It can be useful when uploading photographs to online forms, applications, and websites that have image-size requirements.

Our goal is to make image resizing simple and accessible. Users can upload a supported image, select an available target size, and download the result. The output quality and final file size may vary depending on the original image and its dimensions.
